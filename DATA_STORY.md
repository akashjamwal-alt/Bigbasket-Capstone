# Data Story — BigBasket Category Revenue Analysis

## Summary
Across Jan–Jun 2026, BigBasket's 6 categories generated a combined
₹88,282 in Delivered revenue from 434 orders (average order value:
₹203.4). 3 of 6 categories are meeting or exceeding their monthly
revenue targets; 3 are falling short.

## Category-by-category status

**Above Target (3 categories):**
- **Household Essentials** — ₹21,715 vs. ₹17,000 target (+27.7%), the
  strongest performer by a wide margin.
- **Bakery** — ₹15,410 vs. ₹12,000 target (+28.4%), the single
  best-performing category by percentage.
- **Personal Care** — ₹16,382 vs. ₹15,500 target (+5.7%), meeting
  target but with the thinnest margin among the three.

**Below Target - Watch (1 category):**
- **Dairy & Eggs** — ₹14,090 vs. ₹16,500 target (-14.6% shortfall),
  just inside the 15% watch threshold.

**Below Target - Critical (2 categories):**
- **Snacks & Beverages** — ₹10,895 vs. ₹13,000 target (-16.2% shortfall).
- **Fruits & Vegetables** — ₹9,790 vs. ₹12,000 target (-18.4% shortfall),
  the weakest performer both in absolute revenue and percentage gap.

## Recommendations

**1. Invest catalog/marketing effort in Fruits & Vegetables.**
This category has both the largest percentage shortfall (-18.4%) and
the lowest absolute revenue (₹9,790) of all 6 categories. Since these
are high-frequency, low-margin staple items, even a modest increase in
order volume or basket size here could meaningfully close the gap —
this is a strong candidate for promotional pricing or bundling
campaigns to drive repeat purchases.

**2. Review supplier performance for Snacks & Beverages.**
Despite having the highest order count of any category in the dataset,
Snacks & Beverages still falls short of its target (-16.2%), suggesting
individual order values are too low to hit the revenue goal even with
strong order volume. This points toward a product-mix or pricing issue
rather than a demand issue — worth reviewing whether current suppliers'
price points and SKU selection are competitive enough to lift average
order value in this category.
